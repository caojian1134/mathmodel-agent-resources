# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Distinguish verified facts from personal opinions and project claims.
- Welcome corrections from upstream maintainers and community members.
- Respect licenses, attribution, privacy, security, and contest rules.
- Do not use Issues or Pull Requests for spam, paid promotion, vote manipulation, or live-contest answer sharing.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, or threats;
- Publishing private information without permission;
- Misrepresenting a project as endorsed, certified, or officially affiliated;
- Posting malicious files, secret-stealing instructions, or unsafe commands;
- Repeated promotional content unrelated to maintaining this directory.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns through a private channel listed by the repository owner when available; avoid publishing sensitive details in a public Issue.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and protect the privacy of reporters as far as reasonably possible.
